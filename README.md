# BasicConnect

**Send and receive CNC programs, and keep an eye on your FANUC machines - from your PC.**

BasicConnect connects to your FANUC-controlled machines over your workshop
network. Move programs between the PC and the machine in a couple of clicks, and
see what every machine is doing without walking out to it.

[**Download BasicConnect →**](https://github.com/MysticFrog/BasicConnect/releases/latest)

---

## What you can do

**Move programs**
- See the programs on the machine and the files on your PC side by side
- Drag a program across to send it to the machine, or to save it from the machine
- Open and edit programs before you send them
- You are always asked to confirm before anything is sent to a machine

**Watch your machines** - a window for each machine, open as many as you like
- What the machine is doing, where the axes are, and how hard the spindles are working
- Alarms as they happen, and a history of past alarms
- Tool offsets, work offsets and macro variables
- Which program is running, and how long the machine has been running
- A picture of the machine's screen
- The machine's PMC signals, with their names

**Machines with more than one path** - lathes with two turrets, or machines
with a loader - are handled too. Choose which path to send to, and open a window
for each.

## What you need

- A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC
- FANUC controls from the 16i / 18i / 20i-A series onwards, including 0i and 30i
- The machines connected to your network, with Ethernet enabled on the control

## Installing

1. Download the latest installer from
   [Releases](https://github.com/MysticFrog/BasicConnect/releases/latest).
2. Run it, read and accept the licence agreement, and choose whether to install
   it just for you or for everyone who uses the PC.
3. Open BasicConnect from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

To update, install the new version over the old one. Your machines, settings and
licence are kept.

## Try it free

BasicConnect works without a licence, for as long as you like. You can leave it
running on a real machine all day. Without a licence you can:

- connect to **one** machine
- send or receive **one program at a time**, once every **five minutes**
- use everything except the **PMC** window

A licence removes all of these limits straight away - no restarting.

## Buying a licence

1. Open BasicConnect on the PC you want to license.
2. Press **Licence**, choose how long you want it for, and press **Buy online**.
3. Pay by card on the secure payment page.
4. Your licence arrives by email, usually within minutes, along with your invoice.
5. Back in BasicConnect, press **Licence**, then **Install a licence file**, and
   choose the file from the email.

**Good to know**
- A licence is for **one PC**. It can't be moved to another PC, so buy it on the
  PC you'll use.
- It's a **one-off payment**, not a subscription. Nothing renews and you won't be
  charged again.
- The time starts from the day your licence is issued.
- When a licence runs out, BasicConnect keeps working with the free limits until
  you buy another.

The licence agreement and the terms of sale are shown when you install, and any
time under **About** in BasicConnect.

## Help and problems

**Something not working?**
[Report it here](https://github.com/MysticFrog/BasicConnect/issues/new). It helps
to include:

- which machine and control it happened on
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- a screenshot of any message
- the details from **About → Copy build details** in BasicConnect, pasted in

Reports here can be read by anyone, so please don't post licence files or anything
about your business you'd rather keep private.

**Questions about your licence** - it won't install, you've lost the file, or you
need a copy of your invoice - email **licences@monkeyco.net**. Include the
**This PC** code shown in the Licence window.

## Your privacy

BasicConnect doesn't collect or send anything about you, your programs or your
machines. It checks your licence on your own PC and doesn't need the internet to
do it. Only when you press **Buy online** does it pass a code that identifies the
PC to the payment page, so your licence can be made for it.
